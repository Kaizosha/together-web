01 / ABOUT

About

Together helps a person understand a video in another language without sending the video away.

  1. Choose one or more videos.
  2. Confirm the spoken language and optionally choose a translation language.
  3. Watch with realtime or existing captions.
  4. Review or edit the transcript when needed.
  5. Export captions or a captioned video.

02 / CAPABILITIES

Capabilities

Captions
Use realtime on-device speech transcription, selectable caption tracks already in the video, or supported sidecar captions.
Translation
Show the original, the translation, or both. Translation begins Off until a target language is chosen. Low Latency is the default; High Fidelity appears only when supported and ready.
Export
Save WebVTT, SRT, TTML, text, Markdown, or JSON caption files. Create a new MOV or MP4 with Together captions as a selectable track or rendered into the picture. The source video is untouched.
Platforms
iPhone and iPad / iOS and iPadOS 26 / In development.

03 / PRIVACY

Privacy

Together does not collect personal data. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-report upload, or cloud-sync service. Kaizōsha does not receive the videos, audio, captions, transcripts, language choices, export history, or preferences used in Together.

Media and the text derived from it are processed on the device through Apple system frameworks and are not uploaded to a Together or Kaizōsha server.
